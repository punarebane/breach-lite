## Pitch
Three mechs defend a city grid from burrowing swarms on a 7x7 board. Every enemy shows exactly what it will hit next turn. You have perfect information and too few actions. Push, swap and redirect enemies so they hit each other instead of the city.

## Turn structure (deterministic, no hit rolls)
1. Enemy phase A: enemies move and telegraph their attacks (target tile + direction, with a numbered resolution order). Spawn points for next turn are marked.
2. Player phase: each mech may move, then act, once, in any order. A mech may undo its move until it acts. "Reset turn" is available once per mission.
3. Enemy phase B: telegraphed attacks resolve in order. Attacks hit whatever is on the tile NOW, so pushing an enemy changes where its attack lands.
4. Spawns emerge. A unit standing on a spawn point blocks it and takes 1 damage.

Mission: survive 5 turns (or 4 + objective). Mechs have 3 HP; at 0 they are disabled for the rest of the mission and repaired after it.

## Rules
- Push: moves a unit 1 tile. Blocked by a unit, mountain or building = both take 1 damage (bump). Into water = ground units drown. Into a chasm = non-flying units die.
- Terrain: Ground, Mountain (blocks; 2 hits to destroy), Water, Chasm, Forest (catches fire when hit; burning units take 1 per turn), Building (1-2 grid power each), Objective building (bonus).
- Grid power: starts at 7 per run. Each building hit = -1 grid. 0 = run over. Grid defence: 15% chance a building resists (the only randomness; seeded).
- Mechs (each weapon in data/weapons.json):
  Brawler: move 3; Punch, adjacent, 2 damage, pushes the target away.
  Artillery: move 3; Arc Shell, target 2+ tiles away in a straight line, flies over obstacles, 1 damage to the target, pushes the 4 adjacent tiles outward.
  Warper: move 4, flying; Swap, exchange places with any unit within 3 tiles, no damage.
- Enemies (data/enemies.json; 6 types + boss): Beetle (melee 1, adjacent), Hornet (flying, melee 1), Spitter (projectile, hits the first thing in a line, 1), Scarab (artillery, 2 damage, 2+ tiles), Digger (burrows, emerges anywhere, melee 2), Blob (explodes on death, 1 damage to adjacent tiles), and the Hive Queen boss (spawns larvae, telegraphs a 3-tile line attack).
- Enemy AI: seeded utility scoring over (reachable tile x attack direction): +10 hit a building, +8 hit a mech, +12 hit the objective, -6 hit another enemy, small noise from the seeded RNG. Each enemy plans against the board state after the previous enemies' moves.

## Run structure
- 4 islands x 2 missions + a final 2-phase mission against the Hive Queen.
- Each island has a terrain flavour (forests, water, mountains, chasms) used by a seeded mission generator built on hand-authored template constraints.
- Bonus objectives (kill 7 enemies, protect the objective, take less than 2 mech damage) award Reputation. Between islands spend it on: +1 grid power, a reactor core (weapon upgrade: +1 damage, +1 range, or a weapon-specific effect), or a new weapon from a pool of 12 (each mech has 2 slots).
- Save/continue between missions.

## Architecture (the learning focus)
- core/ holds pure logic as RefCounted classes with NO Node dependencies: Board, Unit, GameState (deep copy via duplicate()), Action, Resolver.
- Resolver.apply(state, action) -> Array[Event] (moved, damaged, pushed, bumped, destroyed, drowned, fire_started, grid_lost...). The presentation layer only plays events back with tweens.
- Attack preview = run the action on a cloned state and render the resulting events as overlays (damage numbers, push arrows, death skulls, building warnings) BEFORE confirming. This is the signature feature; it must exactly match real resolution.
- ai/ for enemy planning; gen/ for mission generation; data/ as JSON (diffable, easy for you to edit); scenes/ for presentation.
- Rendering: top-down 2D board drawn with _draw() or a TileMapLayer, units as Node2D with code-drawn shapes. A clean placeholder look: hard shadows, a strong colour per faction, telegraph lines with order badges. A palette in data/palette.json.
- Input: mouse first; keyboard shortcuts (1/2/3 to select mech, Z to undo, Space to end turn).
- Audio: short SFX generated with AudioStreamGenerator or bundled sfxr-style WAVs generated by a script.

## Tests & simulation
- GUT (addons/gut), run headless: godot --headless -s addons/gut/gut_cmdln.gd -gdir=res://tests -gexit
- Unit tests for every rule: push chains, bumps, drowning, chasm, fire, blob explosion chains, attack resolution order, spawn blocking, grid defence with a fixed seed.
- Property test: for 2,000 random (state, legal action) pairs, the preview events exactly equal the real resolution events.
- Invariant fuzz: a random-legal-action bot plays 500 seeded missions with no errors; invariants hold every step (unique positions, HP within bounds, no unit on a blocking tile).
- Balance sim (tools/sim.gd, headless): a greedy player bot evaluates every move+action combo per mech with a 1-ply heuristic (enemies killed, telegraphed attacks redirected or cancelled, buildings threatened). 300 seeded runs. Report mission win rate per island, grid lost per mission and run win rate. Targets: island-1 mission win rate over 85%, run win rate 10-30%. Tune data/ until they hold; record the results in PROGRESS.md.
- Screenshots: a --screenshot=<fixture> command-line argument loads a fixture state, renders a few frames, saves a PNG via the viewport texture and quits (needs a display; skip gracefully when headless).
